With the stacking lip on, the lid is a flush inset — it drops into the lip recess and its top sits level with the bin rim (uses clearance + lid thickness; skirt/wall apply only to the over-lid used when the lip is off).

Pinch grip (recessed 2-finger)
Stackable lid (inset + lip on top)

Pinch grip sinks two elliptical finger wells into the lid top whose floor is curved along Z: a quarter circle running from ~0 mm at the outer edge to ~15 mm deep at the inner edge facing the other well, so a fingertip slides in shallow and curls down to pinch the central web. Nothing sticks up above the top. Stackable keeps the lid a flush inset (widest part = bin width, not wrapping around) and adds a Gridfinity stacking lip on top so another bin nests. Either way the lid locks into the bin’s stacking-lip recess with ribs on the four straight edges only — the rounded corners are left clear so the lid drops straight in and lifts straight out without the corners binding.
